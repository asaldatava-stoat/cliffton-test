Our
Colours

Each screen is led by one brand colour: orange for Strengths, lavender for the Enneagram, black for everything that holds them together. Blue marks focus, sage keeps surfaces calm. Tints at 10–15% are allowed for large reading areas.

of Voice

  3. Short first. Specific second.
  4. Say the limits once, small, at the bottom.
